## Title
Freeze-Up

## Short description (tagline)
Time is money. Make the last boat out of the Klondike before the river freezes.

## Page text

**Gold rush, Canada, 1898.** The last steamboat home leaves Dawson before the Yukon River freezes, and a ticket costs **270 gold**. You have 10 gold, 28 hours, and a revolver.

Roam the frozen wilds, fight wolves, bandits and bears for bounties, and trade your own time for money, or your money for time, before the river locks you in for the winter.

### Time is money
- **Trade time and gold with your own hands.** Hold **E** to buy hours with gold, or **Q** to work for gold. Prices rise as the river freezes, and trading back always loses. You can't win without trading, so choose your moment.
- **The bounty clock.** Win a fight fast and you get the full bounty. Take too long and it drops.
- **The clock never stops.** Walking, shopping, training and fighting all cost time, and heavy gold slows you down.

### Features
- An open world with five unique places, each with three tiers of harder, better-paid fights and a boss at the end.
- Enemies that warn before they attack: bandits shout "!", wolves crouch before they lunge, bears slam the ground and rage at half health.
- Lose a fight and half the lost gold waits for you to win it back.
- Optional training at the Shooting Range: faster shots, faster reloads and critical hits.
- **Map codes:** share a code and friends play the exact same world.
- **Easy / Normal / Hard** difficulty.

### Controls
| | |
|---|---|
| Move | WASD / arrow keys |
| Enter a place or shop | Space |
| Aim / shoot | Mouse / click (one shot per click; quick clicks spread your shots) |
| Dash | Space (in fights) |
| Reload / weapons | R / 1–3 |
| Work (time → gold) | hold Q |
| Buy time (gold → time) | hold E |
| Pause / mute | P / M |

A run takes about 6–8 minutes. Play with keyboard and mouse.
